### 道歉信

**Suppose you have to cancel your travel plan and will not be able to visit Professor Smith.** 

**Write him an email to**：

1)apologize and explain the situation, and

2)suggest a future meeting. 

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. 

Do not use your own name. Use “Li Ming” instead. 

Do not write your address. (10 points)

注意语气措辞

第一段改写题干,换词，多几个同义词

I must apologize for change/withdrawing/postpone from my scheduled visit/meeting. 

The appointment was one I had long anticipated,yet a sudden family emergency now makes the trip impossible.

整段写完看过去式，三单等

第二段，讲具体因为什么事情

Earlier this week, news arrived that a close family member had suddenly fallen seriously ill. 

The situation has made my presence at home unavoidable,and in such circumstances proceeding with the trip would mean neglecting a responsibility that can not reasonably be set aside. 

For that reason, the visit must be regrettably postponed, so I hope another opportunity to meet can be arranged once the situation stabilizes.

第三段再一次的道歉

My sincere apologies again for any inconvenience that this change of plan may cause.

> I sincerely apologize again for any inconvenience **caused by** the change of plan.

### 校园话题投稿

学校存在什么不好的现象，请你向英文报投稿

第一段：具体描述一下子+反应了我们各位缺乏了......素养

第二段：......重要性，没有......会怎么样，正反论证+因此我们要怎么做

> 分析原因，为什么会产生这种现象

第三段：呼吁怎么做


你校的英语报正在开展以“Teamwork”为主题的征文活动。请你针对你们班学习小组合作中部分成员不出力的“划水”现象，写一篇短文投稿，内容包括：

1. 描述具体现象
2. 提出你的建议

**Free-riding in Teamwork : A Problem to Solve**

第一段

It is difficult to imagine to that such conduct could occur on a campus there team spirit is expected to flourish/a campus that champions teamwork.

In group projects, while some students invest genuine effort,others contribute little.

> 最近有个不好是事情发生/有些学生注意到了.......。Such free-riding exposes a troubling absence of responsibility and team spirit.

Such behavior ultimately reveals a troubling absence of responsibility and team sprit.

第二段

If everyone shrank from their share of the word, those bearing most of the workload would soon feel unfairly treated and lose the willingness to cooperate.

> If everyone lacked team spirit and shirked responsibility, trust would quickly collapse and even the simplest task would become impossible.

Teamwork,therefore,is not merely a slogan but a call for conscious action.

Each member should treat it as a shared commitment : finish assigned tasks on time , offering ideas in discussion, and engaging actively in problem- solving.

第三段

Only through shared effort can genuine progress take place. Let us act accordingly.

> Only when every one shoulders their share can we create a campus defined by responsibility and mutual support.
