## 图表作文

### 描述部分

#### 动态

通常表现为折线图，随时间变化等
##### 多对象

The chart/table above compares changes in [图表主题] of A、B and C in [对象] from [开始时间] to [结束时间]

The table above compares changes in the ownership of air conditioners, washing machines, and refrigerators per 100 households in China from 2014 to 2023.
> 近年来全国居民平均每百户年末主要耐用消费品拥有量


To be specific,from [时间1] to [时间2], [A] experienced a sharp increase / decline, rising / falling from [数值1] to [数值2].  

> 具体来看，从[时间1]到[时间2]，[A]经历了明显的上升/下降趋势，由[数值1]上升/下降至[数值2]。

Meanwhile, [B] displayed a more gradual upward / downward trend, increasing / decreasing from [数值3] to [数值4].

> 与此同时，[B]则呈现出较为平缓的增长/下降态势，从[数值3]增加/减少到[数值4]。

By contrast, [C] remained relatively stable, with only minor fluctuations during the same period.

>相比之下，[C]在同一时期基本保持稳定，仅出现小幅波动。

##### 单对象

The chart/table above illustrates changes in [图表主题]+对象 over/from [开始时间] to [结束时间]

The line chart above illustrates changes in health literacy levels among Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021.

To be specific,from [时间1] to [时间2], [A] experienced a sharp increase / decline, rising / falling from [数值1] to [数值2].  

Meanwhile, [B] displayed a more gradual upward / downward trend, increasing / decreasing from [时间3] to [时间4].

如果只有上升或下降，写其中一句就行

#### 静态

没有变化，通常是一些扇形图，饼图之类的

The chart/table above presents the composition of [整体] in 哪里+时间（题目进行转换）
The chart above presents the composition of factors influencing consumers choice of restaurants in a certain city in 2017

To be specific, [A] accounts for the largest / smallest proportion, standing at approximately [数值1], and thus ranks first / last.  
>具体来看，[A]所占比例最大/最小，约为[数值1]，因此位居首位/末位。

[B] follows, making up around [数值2], showing a similar / slightly higher / slightly lower level.  

> B]紧随其后，占比约为[数值2]，整体水平相近/略高/略低。

By comparison, [C] and [D] remain at comparable levels, at roughly [数值3] and [数值4] respectively.  

> 相比之下，[C]和[D]的占比处于相近水平，分别约为[数值3]和[数值4]。

Overall, the proportion tends to increase / decrease across different groups, with the highest level observed in [某一组].

> 总体而言，不同群体之间的占比呈现出上升/下降趋势，其中[某一组]所占比例最高。

These changes are not merely statistical, but indicative of a wider social trend.

> 这些变化并非只是统计层面的波动，而是反映出更广泛的社会发展趋势。

Behind the numerical changes lies a deeper transformation in society.

> 在数字变化的背后，蕴含着社会更深层次的转型。

It is difficult to imagine to that such conduct could occur on a campus there team spirit is expected to flourish/a campus that champions teamwork.

In group projects, while some students invest genuine effort,others contribute little.

> 最近有个不好是事情发生/有些学生注意到了.......。Such free-riding exposes a troubling absence of responsibility and team spirit.

Such behavior ultimately reveals a troubling absence of responsibility and team sprit.

第二段

> If everyone lacked team spirit and shirked responsibility, trust would quickly collapse and even the simplest task would become impossible.

> Only when every one shoulders their share can we create a campus defined by responsibility and mutual support.
